### UI

- The instructions were a bit confusing. Shall we have suggestions for making
  instructions clearer (reading level etc.)?

### Uploading work

- People were asked to upload their work as a photo, which was difficult to get
  users to do (motivation).
- When users uploaded a picture, it didn't upload from the phone, they didn't see
  the image, and they couldn't make it bigger. Look into these issues.

### Tutor role

- Teaching and exploration are muddled for students: what exactly is the role?
- Henrik responded as if it were a numerical problem. How [would it respond to it]
  as an exploration problem?
- Make it easier to give teachers tools to compare not just teaching approaches but
  the role, or the conversation.

### Research instrumentation

- Audio recordings should be able to marry up their timestamps with the actions
  students take in the chat transcripts.
- When a student does certain actions, a survey can pop up (e.g. "it's an
  embarrassing question, what did you feel?").
- Experience momentum analysis, when you ask "what is n".

### Live view

- The live view worked, but it says "Stuck" rather than "not active".
- If a student is asking the question several different ways, that could be stuck.
  So: more diagnostics for the live groups.

### Worksheets, sims and activities

- Interactive worksheet vs simulations: features already in the workbench were put
  into the simulation.
- Click on it so it progressively advances the exercise. Could that be a new
  activity in a chain?
- Student simulation creation.
- Make simulations that are wrong, and students critique them.
- https://phet.colorado.edu/ is an example. It started as research.

### Classroom dynamics (research questions)

- Is the teacher being replaced?
- Is it possible to integrate AI for teachers and students in a way that is
  meaningful?
- What are the dynamics of a classroom with AI involved? AI on behalf of the
  student? AI on behalf of the teacher?
- Talking vs typing. Are we learning with a lively classroom using talk vs text?
- The tutor being proactive and interrupting.
- Take away the chat box and the interactive workbench, and use pointing instead.
- Is AI a tool or an artefact?
- The culture of the classroom will shape the dynamics.

### Priorities

- Will we review the workshop activity and work on the UI as priorities?
