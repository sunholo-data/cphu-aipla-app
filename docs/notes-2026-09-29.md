### ⚠️ The finding that explains two complaints at once

*"Different tutor personas sometimes gave the same answers"* and *"is the tutor
based on the teaching approach? not convinced"* have a **structural** explanation.
**All seven base persona tutors carry `frameworkId: null` by design**
(`backend/tutors/*.yaml`; tutors handover rule 2: a persona–approach pairing is a
pedagogical claim nobody has signed off). A persona changes name, avatar, voice and
register. **It does not change the teaching approach.** Two personas with no framework
*should* teach alike. The approach only arrives through a **variant** (1.1.135 lets
teachers make one). BENCH-1/2 showed the approaches do differ when present, modestly
(4–5 of 7), so the question to ask JB is **which tutors the session used: base personas,
or variants with an approach?** This matters for **the 30 Sep research-group demo**
("different tutor personas on a wave sim"). If it used base personas, it will show the
same pedagogy in different voices.
