## The seminar in one page

- **Shared group codes may be leaking chat history between people and between
  activities, and that history may be shaping the tutor's answers where nobody can
  see it.** This is the most serious finding, because it is both a correctness
  problem and a research-validity problem.
- **A teacher deleted her group codes, and the researcher can no longer review
  those sessions**, even though the data still exists.
- **The tutor did not direct students to the workbench or the simulations.** It
  stayed in pure dialogue. This is the integration the 29 Sept meeting called the
  platform's whole point.
- **The assessment view needs more transparency:** how the turn references and the
  judgements (partial, strong) are computed, and whether researchers can correct them.
- Rendering and UX defects: LaTeX in chat, a misspelt table heading ("opsamling"),
  chat not auto-scrolling, and documents that cannot be clicked open.
- **An unexplained "Didaktisk tutor" appeared in Aswin's account.**
- Teachers' questions were mostly about **rights and scope**: copyright, GDPR,
  local models, other subjects, the rollout schedule. **Miira** (miira.dk, "AI til
  læring") was raised as a comparable product.

**Found while writing them (outside the seminar's items, not yet designed):**

- **`/api/reports/*` has no ownership check.** Any authenticated caller can read
  any group's session report, and that includes a student holding a group token.
  `backend/protocols/reports_routes.py` says so in its own docstring: *"today every
  authenticated user can read every report — fine for LOCAL_MODE's single-teacher
  world"*. Prod is not that world. This needs its own fix, ahead of the six docs.
- **A revoked group code's live token keeps authenticating** until it expires,
  because verification never reads Firestore. A deleted code string can also be
  minted again. Both are recorded in 1.1.146.
- The privacy notice deployed on 10-05 (`2a1d0619`) sits above every student chat
  and shrinks the scroll area (1.1.147, open question).

**Not designed:** the teachers' questions. They are questions to answer, not defects
to fix. Several already have answers on the public site
(`https://aipla.ku.dk/project/data-and-hosting`, `/project/activities`,
`/project/build-a-simulation`). A short teacher FAQ assembled from those pages is the
cheap follow-up. Rights in teacher-uploaded work and GDPR for group IDs need M's and
the university's position first. Do not let an agent write those answers.
